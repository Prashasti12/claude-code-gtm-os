# KOL DM Writer

Writes DMs that read like a person wrote them, short enough to answer from a phone.

## Inputs

- Creator name, niche and one recent post of theirs worth referencing
- Project name and one-liner
- Offer: paid collab, rev share, ambassador, early access
- Tone: casual native or professional

## Rules

- Five lines maximum.
- Open with something specific about their work, never "hope you're doing well".
- One clear ask and one easy next step.
- No corporate words, no hype, no fake urgency.

## Output

Two versions, one casual and one slightly more formal, plus a one-line follow-up to send after three days of silence.
