# KOL Researcher

Builds a shortlist of creators who actually fit the project, not just big accounts.

## Inputs

- Project name and category (DeFi, AI, creator economy, consumer, etc.)
- Target audience
- Platform
- Budget tier: nano, micro, mid or macro

## Steps

1. Describe the audience the project needs, then work backwards to the creators that audience already follows.
2. If an X research tool is available (for example the open-source x-research skill), search recent posts on the category and pull profiles of the most engaged authors. Otherwise use web search and say so.
3. Drop accounts whose recent posts are mostly giveaways, paid shills or off-topic.
4. Score each remaining creator on fit, engagement quality and likely cost for the budget tier.

## Output

A table of 10 creators:

| Handle | Followers | Niche | Why they fit | Engagement style | Outreach angle |
|--------|-----------|-------|--------------|------------------|----------------|

Flag any follower counts you could not verify. Never invent numbers.
