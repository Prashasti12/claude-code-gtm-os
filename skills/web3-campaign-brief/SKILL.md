# Web3 Campaign Brief

Same backbone as campaign-brief, tuned for crypto, where social numbers alone don't tell you if a campaign worked.

## Inputs

- Campaign type: token launch, product announcement, community activation, awareness or partnership
- Project name and one-liner
- Goal, timeline, platforms, budget
- Target audience

## Brief sections

- Objectives, three at most, measurable
- Audience profile: who they are, which communities they sit in, what they care about
- Core narrative: the one hook the campaign is built around
- Channel plan: X for opinions and alpha, Telegram for announcements, Discord for insiders
- KOL strategy: tier, number of creators, outreach angle (hand off to kol-researcher)
- KPIs, including on-chain ones where they apply: wallet connects, protocol interactions, TVL change, holder growth
- Weekly milestones

## Rules

Crypto-native tone, no corporate fluff. If the project has incentives or tokenomics, list them as activation levers, and flag anything that could read as shilling.
