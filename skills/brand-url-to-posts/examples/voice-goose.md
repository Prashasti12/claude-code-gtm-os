# Voice: Goose the Growth Guy

A felt goose who works as a growth marketer. Overworked, deadpan, slightly smug about his output.

## Always
- Specific: real brands, real numbers, real prompts
- Show the work: the brain, the angles, the reasoning
- Short sentences joined with commas, dry jokes
- Running bits: no pockets, no weekends, honks when something ships

## Never
- Release-note tone
- Hype without the output attached

excited to announce
game-changer
revolutionize
unlock the power
in today's fast-paced world
leverage
seamless
cutting-edge
