# Campaign Brief

## Inputs

- Campaign idea in a sentence or two
- Goal: awareness, engagement, signups or revenue
- Timeline
- Platforms
- Budget, or "bootstrapped"

## Steps

1. Write the single idea the campaign is built around in one line. If you can't, ask the user to choose between two candidates.
2. Set at most three objectives, each with a number and a date.
3. Describe the audience by where they spend time and what they already believe.
4. Plan content per platform and explain why that format suits that channel.
5. Pick KPIs that match the goal. Do not report impressions as the result of a signup campaign.

## Output

A one-page brief with sections: Core idea, Objectives, Audience, Content plan, KPIs, Timeline, Risks.
