# Performance Summary

## Inputs

- Platform and time period
- Raw metrics: impressions, engagement, followers, clicks, top posts, spend if any
- Last period's numbers, if available

## Steps

1. Compare with the previous period. Without a baseline, say so instead of calling something a win.
2. Separate vanity metrics from outcomes. Followers and impressions are inputs, signups and revenue are outcomes.
3. Look at the top three and bottom three posts and name what they have in common: format, hook, timing or topic.
4. Turn each pattern into a test for next period.

## Output

Under one page:

- Headline: one sentence on how the period went
- Wins, with numbers
- Learnings, each tied to evidence
- Three next steps, each written as a test with a success metric
